Questions

Community — frequently asked

1

Are Community trips solo-friendly?

Yes. Community circles hold solo seats on purpose — arrive alone, eat with people who came for the same reason. Coorg, Kutch, and Meghalaya from ₹30,000.

2

How many people are in a Community circle?

Never more than ten chairs at the table. A host, a kitchen, and a small group — not a tour bus.

3

Which destinations do you run Community trips to?

Coorg, Kutch, and Meghalaya are the core Community circles. Each trip page shows duration, from price, and what is included.

4

How do I book a solo seat?

Open a Community trip page, check seats if listed, then request via the form or WhatsApp. The Hyderabad desk confirms personally.
