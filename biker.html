Questions

Biker — frequently asked

1

What is included in a Manali to Leh bike trip?

A serviced motorcycle or yours in the convoy, support vehicle with mechanic and spare parts, fuel and permits, a road captain, stays that expect dust, and daily breakfast.

2

Do I need my own motorcycle?

No. We provide a serviced bike, or you can ride your own in the convoy. High Road Leh and other biker journeys list exact inclusions on each trip page.

3

Is there a support vehicle and mechanic?

Yes. Every biker journey includes a vehicle behind the group with a mechanic, spare parts, and a road captain who has ridden the pass in weather.

4

Can I book from Hyderabad?

Yes. Request any biker trip via WhatsApp +91 99511 39299 or the booking form. The desk is in Hitech City, Hyderabad, open 10:00–18:00 IST.
