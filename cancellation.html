Policies

Cancellation policy

Clear windows so you know what a hold costs to release. Trip requests saved in your browser can be released anytime from My bookings.

Live desk bookings

  • 45+ days before departure — full credit toward another Loop trip, or refund per the refund policy.
  • 30–44 days — 50% of the trip value held as credit; remainder may be retained for supplier holds.
  • Under 30 days — non-refundable except for documented medical or force-majeure cases reviewed by the desk.
  • Surprise sealed trips — same windows; destination reveal does not change cancellation rights.

How to cancel

Write to concierge@looptrips.com or WhatsApp +91 99511 39299 with your Loop reference. We confirm in writing within one business day.

Browser-saved requests

Trip requests created on this website are saved in your browser for your reference. No payment is taken online. Release them anytime under My bookings, or WhatsApp the desk to confirm a live hold.
