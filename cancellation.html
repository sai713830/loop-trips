Cancellation policy

Clear windows and charges so you know the cost of releasing a confirmed hold. This policy applies once the desk has confirmed your itinerary and accepted payment or a binding hold in writing.

Effective: 1 September 2026 Applies to: Confirmed desk bookings Related: Refund policy

1. Definitions

  • Departure date — the first day of travel as stated on your written confirmation or invoice.
  • Trip value — the total package amount confirmed by Loop Trips, excluding personal extras paid directly to vendors unless otherwise stated.
  • Written confirmation — email or WhatsApp message from the desk with your Loop reference, dates, inclusions, and payment status.
  • Supplier components — hotels, flights, trains, cruises, activities, and transfers booked in your name with third parties.

2. Standard cancellation schedule

Cancellation charges below apply to the trip value once a booking is confirmed. Where a supplier imposes a higher non-refundable charge, that amount is deducted in addition.

Notice before departure Cancellation charge Refund / credit
45 days or more Administrative fee up to 10% of trip value, or actual supplier penalties — whichever is higher Balance as refund or full credit toward another Loop trip within 12 months
30 – 44 days 50% of trip value Remaining 50% as credit toward another Loop trip within 12 months, unless refund is approved under the refund policy
15 – 29 days 75% of trip value 25% as credit at desk discretion; refund only for documented medical or force-majeure cases
Under 15 days 100% of trip value No refund except documented medical or force-majeure cases reviewed in writing by the desk
No-show / on departure day 100% of trip value No refund

Days are counted in calendar days from the date we acknowledge your cancellation request to the departure date. Peak-season windows (Diwali, Christmas–New Year, long weekends) may carry stricter supplier terms — the desk will flag these on your confirmation.

3. Supplier-specific components

Many bookings include non-refundable elements. Cancellation of these follows the supplier’s policy in addition to the schedule above:

  • Flights & trains — governed by airline or rail fare rules once tickets are issued.
  • Hotels & houseboats — peak dates and special offers may be 100% non-refundable from the date of booking.
  • Cruises, ferries & boats — including services to islands such as Havelock; weather or operator cancellation does not entitle a refund beyond what the operator returns.
  • Permits & activity vouchers — often non-refundable once applied for or issued.

Loop Trips passes through supplier recoveries where possible. We do not absorb third-party penalties beyond amounts we can reclaim.

4. Partial cancellation & name changes

  • Removing one guest from a group booking is treated as a partial cancellation. Charges apply per person removed, based on the schedule above.
  • Name changes may be permitted on flights and some hotels for a fee, subject to supplier rules. They are not guaranteed.
  • Date changes are treated as cancellation plus rebooking, not as a free amendment.

5. Trip types

Surprise sealed trips

Same cancellation windows apply. Destination reveal does not alter your cancellation rights or charges.

Group & community departures

If a minimum group size is not met, Loop Trips may reschedule or cancel the departure. In that case you receive a full refund of amounts paid to Loop or the option to transfer to another date.

International journeys

Visa fees, insurance, and long-haul flights often carry stricter terms. Your confirmation will note any non-refundable portions before payment.

6. Force majeure & natural events

If travel is prevented or curtailed by natural calamity, severe weather, epidemic orders, strikes, or government restrictions, Loop Trips will work with suppliers to secure credits, rescheduling, or refunds to the extent recoverable from vendors. We cannot guarantee full recovery where suppliers declare force majeure.

Itinerary changes made by Loop Trips for operational or safety reasons are described in our Terms & conditions. Those changes alone do not automatically qualify for a cash refund unless the desk agrees in writing.

7. Complimentary activities

For any activity that is complimentary and not charged to Loop Trips or the guest, no refund or compensation will be processed if that activity cannot take place.

8. How to cancel

  1. Email looptripsindia@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 99511 39299 with your Loop reference, lead guest name, and reason for cancellation.
  2. We acknowledge within one business day (10:00–18:00 IST, Monday–Saturday) and confirm applicable charges.
  3. Approved refunds are processed per the refund policy. Credits are issued by email with a validity date.

Cancellation is effective from the timestamp of our written acknowledgement, not from the date you first message us.

9. Browser-saved trip requests

Trip requests created on this website are saved in your browser for your reference. No payment is taken online. Release them anytime under My bookings, or WhatsApp the desk to convert to a live hold. This cancellation policy applies only after the desk confirms a paid or binding hold.

Before you pay. Ask the desk which components of your itinerary are non-refundable. We will highlight peak-season hotel blocks, issued tickets, and permit-dependent activities on your confirmation.
