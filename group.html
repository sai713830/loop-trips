Questions

Group — frequently asked

1

Do you plan private group trips from Hyderabad?

Yes. Group journeys are for eight to twenty-four guests — one person books, everyone else arrives. Haveli, Konkan house, Himalayan ridge, and other routes show a from price in ₹ on each trip page.

2

How many people can travel in a private group?

Typically eight to twenty-four chairs depending on the house or route. Each Group trip page lists typical group size and minimum guests. The desk confirms capacity for your dates.

3

What is included in a group journey?

Stays that fit the whole table, private transfers, a journey designer on Indian hours, daily breakfast, and all listed experiences on the trip page. Flights are extra unless noted.

4

Can one person book for the whole group?

Yes. One contact holds the week — dates, headcount, and budget. We send a single itinerary and from price; the desk reviews every request before confirming. No payment on this website.
