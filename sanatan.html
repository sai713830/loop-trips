Questions

Sanatan — frequently asked

1

Do you offer Char Dham yatra packages from Hyderabad?

Yes. Char Dham Path and other Sanatan journeys are planned from our Hyderabad desk with route, darshan windows, and a from price in ₹ on each trip page.

2

What is included in a Sanatan journey?

Stays chosen for the hour they keep, private transfers, a journey designer on Indian hours, daily breakfast, and all listed rites, tickets, and guides named in the plan.

3

How is this different from temple tourism?

Teerth is a sequence, not a checklist. We time the river, the door, and the hour — darshan windows held before the public rush, with a scholar walking with you.

4

When is the best time for temple yatras?

Each trip page lists best months. Char Dham and Himalayan routes typically run April–June and September–October; the desk confirms dates for your party.
