Questions

Frequently asked

1

How do I book a trip with Loop Trips?

Open any trip page, check the from price and day-by-day plan, then request a trip via the form or WhatsApp. Our Hyderabad desk reviews every request personally. No payment is taken on this website.

2

What does the from price mean?

Every trip shows a starting price in ₹ per person — the floor, not a locked quote. Classic, Prestige, Royal, or a custom ceiling via the concierge can reshape stays, pace, and cities.

3

Where is the Loop Trips desk?

Hitech City, Madhapur, Hyderabad. Open 10:00–18:00 IST. WhatsApp +91 99511 39299 for the fastest reply.

4

What types of trips do you plan?

Sanatan temple yatras, biker routes, community solo circles, solo itineraries, surprise destinations, private groups, and international Spice Route Luxe journeys.

5

Can I customise a trip to my budget?

Yes. Tell the concierge your ceiling — for example ₹60,000 in Kerala or ₹2L for Bali — and we reshape the itinerary to what you can spend.
