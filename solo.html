Questions

Solo — frequently asked

1

Do you plan solo trips from Hyderabad?

Yes. Solo journeys are planned from our Hyderabad desk with single occupancy as default — Himachal, Kerala, Gokarna, and other routes on each trip page show a from price in ₹.

2

Is single occupancy included in the from price?

Yes. Solo trip pages list a from price per person with your own room unless the page notes a shared stay. Tell the desk your ceiling and we reshape the week.

3

How is Solo different from Community?

Solo is a private itinerary — one guest, one plan. Community holds solo seats at a shared table with eight to ten people. Both start from ₹30,000; choose the fit on the trip page.

4

How do I book a solo trip?

Open any Solo trip page, read the day-by-day plan, then request via the form or WhatsApp. Our desk reviews every request personally. No payment is taken on this website.
