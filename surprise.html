Questions

Surprise — frequently asked

1

How does a surprise destination trip work?

You pick the month, rooms, and pace — the place stays sealed until forty-eight hours before you fly. Loop Trips plans the route from our Hyderabad desk; the envelope opens when you are already packed.

2

Can I choose the destination for a surprise trip?

No — that is the point. You choose land, water, or both; still or moving days; who travels; and how long. We match a sealed journey from our Surprise list and reveal it two days before departure.

3

What is included in a surprise journey?

Stays, listed transfers, and experiences named in the plan once the envelope opens. Flights to the start city are extra unless noted. Every surprise trip shows a from price in ₹ on its page.

4

When do I learn where I am going?

Forty-eight hours before you fly. Until then you hold the terms — duration, from price, inclusions — without the place name. The desk confirms dates and budget before you hold.
