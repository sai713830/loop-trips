Privacy policy

Loop Trips respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you enquire, plan, or travel with us — and how we use, share, and protect it.

Effective: 1 September 2026 Data controller: Loop Trips, Hitech City, Hyderabad Privacy contact: concierge@looptrips.com

1. Who we are

Loop Trips operates a travel desk from Hitech City, Madhapur, Hyderabad, Telangana 500081, India. We arrange private and small-group journeys across India and internationally. Full contact details appear on our About and Contact pages.

2. Scope

This policy applies to personal data collected through:

  • This website — trip requests, enquiry forms, and browsing.
  • WhatsApp, email, phone, and in-person communication with our desk.
  • Bookings and travel services we arrange on your behalf.

3. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Identity & contact — name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, postal address, nationality.
  • Travel details — destination interests, dates, group size, budget, passport or ID details where needed for bookings, date of birth or age for fare pricing.
  • Preferences — dietary needs, room type, pace, accessibility requirements, and other notes for itinerary planning.
  • Payment-related data — billing name, transaction references, and amounts. We do not collect or store card numbers on this website.
  • Communications — messages, feedback, and complaints you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring URL, through standard hosting logs and analytics where enabled.
  • Local storage — trip requests and preferences (such as currency display) saved in your browser under My bookings.

4. How we use your information

We process personal data to:

  • Design, price, confirm, and deliver your itinerary.
  • Communicate with you before, during, and after your journey.
  • Issue invoices, holds, vouchers, and supplier bookings.
  • Meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • Improve our trips, website, and customer service.
  • Send marketing about Loop Trips journeys only where you have opted in or where permitted by law. You may opt out at any time.

5. Legal basis for processing

Depending on context, we rely on:

  • Contract — to perform the travel services you request.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate our desk, prevent fraud, and improve services, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where record-keeping or disclosure is required by law.
  • Consent — for optional marketing or where explicitly requested.

6. Sharing your information

We share guest names and travel details only as needed to deliver your trip:

  • Hotels, homestays, houseboats, and other accommodation providers.
  • Airlines, railways, transfer companies, and cruise or ferry operators.
  • Guides, activity operators, and local ground partners.
  • Payment processors, banks, and professional advisers where required.
  • Authorities when legally obliged — for example, permit applications or regulatory requests.

We require suppliers to handle data appropriately for the services they provide. We do not sell your personal data to third parties for their marketing.

7. International transfers

For international journeys, your data may be shared with suppliers outside India (for example, hotels or airlines in your destination country). We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards where data leaves India.

8. Retention

  • Browser-saved trip requests remain on your device until you clear them or remove them under My bookings.
  • Desk records — booking correspondence, invoices, and guest details — are retained as long as needed to fulfil the booking, handle disputes, and meet tax and legal requirements, typically up to seven years for financial records.
  • Marketing preferences are kept until you withdraw consent or ask us to delete them.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data — access controls, secure communication channels, and limited staff access on a need-to-know basis. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete data where we no longer have a lawful reason to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time.
  • Data portability where technically feasible and required by law.

To exercise these rights, write to concierge@looptrips.com or WhatsApp +91 99511 39299. We respond within a reasonable period, usually within 30 days.

11. Cookies & local storage

This site may store preferences — such as currency display and saved trip requests — in your browser’s local storage. We do not use cookies to collect payment card data. You can clear stored data through your browser settings or My bookings.

12. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16 booking independently. We collect children’s data only as provided by a parent or guardian for family travel arrangements.

13. Third-party links

Our site may link to Instagram, WhatsApp, maps, or supplier websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page with the effective date above is the current one. Material changes will be reflected here; continued use of our services after an update constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.

15. Contact & complaints

For privacy questions or complaints, contact us at concierge@looptrips.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority in your jurisdiction.
