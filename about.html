About

A small desk. India first — then anywhere on the map.

Loop Trips plans private itineraries for Indian travellers: Sanatan, biker, community, solo, surprise, group — plus the world. Every trip shows a from price in ₹. India starts at ₹50,000. International starts at ₹1,00,000.

What you get

A finished itinerary, not a pile of hotels.

Each trip page shows a from price in ₹, duration, the city order, typical group size, best months, what is included, and what is not. That number is the starting point — Classic, Prestige, Royal, or a custom ceiling via the concierge.

Indian packages lead the site. Community circles hold solo seats on purpose. International journeys — Bali, Maldives, Dubai, Europe, Japan and beyond — sit right after. Put a finger on the map; we plan the rest.

Booking on this site is a working demo: dates, guests, stay level, a total in ₹, and a Loop reference stored in your browser. No payment is taken. The desk is on Lavelle Road, Bengaluru, 10:00–18:00 IST.
